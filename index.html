• Apple

    APPLE

    A round or slightly oblate pome fruit featuring a smooth, waxy skin that ranges in color from vibrant ruby red
    and golden yellow to bright emerald green. Inside, its pale cream or off-white flesh is firm, crisp, and juicy,
    encasing a small central core with dark brown seeds. The taste balances refreshing tartness with natural sweetness,
    delivering a sharp, satisfying crunch with every bite.










  • Orange

    ORANGE

    A vibrant, spherical citrus fruit featuring a thick, pebbled peel that ranges from bright golden-yellow to deep orange.
    Beneath the outer rind lies a white, fibrous pith protecting tender segments filled with translucent, juice-laden vesicles.
    It delivers a refreshing burst of tangy citrus flavor balanced by a soft, natural sweetness and an invigorating, fresh aroma.










  • Banana

    BANANA

    An elongated, naturally curved tropical berry featuring a smooth, thick peel that transitions from vibrant green to bright
    yellow—and eventually speckled brown—as it ripens. Peeling away the skin reveals a soft, cream-colored fruit with a smooth,
    velvety texture. It delivers a gentle, honeyed sweetness accented by a distinctive tropical aroma.
